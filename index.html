Across = time

A moving playhead reads your drawing from left to right.

Up = higher pitch

Horizontal lines hold a pitch. Curves make glides. Stacked lines sound together.

Brighter = stronger

Soft strokes make quieter layers. Wide strokes add more nearby frequencies.

The drawing defines a target spectrum. The result below measures the synthesized audio; fine details blur with time and frequency resolution. A picture’s shape makes the sound—it doesn’t imitate the object pictured.

SpectraCanvas is a free, open-source spectrogram painter. Draw lines and shapes, or import an image, and hear it as sound: across is time, up is pitch, brighter is louder. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.
